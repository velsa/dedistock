      How to read the stock label

      Hosts tell us different things.

      In stock
      The host's stock system says you can order now.
      Likely
      The host says available, with no guarantee.
      Listed
      On the host's price page. The host gives no stock signal.
      Not confirmed
      Our last good check of this host is over 2 hours old.

      Prices and plan details are quoted from each host's own website. Every row links to the source. Prices can change before you order.

      CPU speed: multi-core render speed from Blender Open Data (CC0), median of public benchmark runs. Higher is faster. An estimate for the CPU model, not a test of this server. No score for dual-CPU servers.

      Setup fee: a one-time fee some hosts charge on the first month. OVH charges about one month's price when you pay monthly without a contract (no fee if you pay 12 months up front). "First year" = 12 months plus the setup fee. Leaseweb does not publish its setup fee in its data.

      OVH lists only servers ready in about 1 hour. Hetzner auction prices include the IPv4 address. Netcup prices are for a 12-month term.
